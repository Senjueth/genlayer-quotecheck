QuoteCheck

Did they really say that? Check the source.

Paste a quote, who it is attributed to and the page it cites. Validators each read that page and must agree on what it shows; the verdict and the page's sha256 stay on-chain for anyone to look up.

contract 0xc109…C101 ↗testnet bradburysource ↗
  1. 01Submit

    The quote, the speaker, the https page it cites and, optionally, where the quote was published.

  2. 02Validators read

    Each fetches the page. Word-for-word presence is checked in code; they agree on meaning and on who the page says said it.

  3. 03Verdict

    Derived in code from what they agreed and stored with the page hash. Anyone can look it up later.
